Impetus is a prestigious global brand that produces underwear, boxers, pyjamas and beachwear for men, women and children. It stands out for its quality, technology and design with certified and patented products.

Since 1973, the brand has become one of the world's leading companies in the textile sector. Today, through its website, it sells all over the world and dispatches its products in less than 24 hours from its factory in Europe.
